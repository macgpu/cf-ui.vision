10x the limits — what that means in practice

The AI assistant does real work for every request: it reads the live page, writes the macro, runs it, reads the log and iterates until the macro passes. Each of those steps costs AI processing. With PRO you get ten times the processing budget of the free plan, so you can:

  • Work through long automation sessions instead of stopping halfway.
  • Let the assistant fix-and-retry a stubborn macro until it really works.
  • Use screenshots and element captures freely — vision requests are the expensive ones.
  • Build bigger macros, with more commands and more page inspection per macro.
